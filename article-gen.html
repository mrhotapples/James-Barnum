Article Gen

Fill in the details, write the article, and this tool builds the finished page in articles/ and adds it to the blog list (blog-data.js). Using the same title again updates that article instead of duplicating it.

This browser can't write to your folder directly (use Chrome or Edge for that). Download mode is on: load your current blog-data.js so the new article gets added to the existing list. You'll download the new files and drop them into your site folder.

New Article

Becomes articles/<slug>.html

Bio and photo come from AUTHORS in article.js.

Copied to images/blog/. Wide (16:9) images look best.

Plain text with light formatting: ## heading, ### subheading, > quote, - bullets, ![alt](path), **bold**, *italic*, [text](url). Image paths are relative to the site root.

Existing Articles

Connect your folder (or load blog-data.js) to see the list.
